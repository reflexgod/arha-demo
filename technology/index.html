Technology

The equipment behind your care, following your visit: how we see, plan, treat and keep you safe.

See more

Diagnosis and imaging

Panoramic X-ray, in-house

What it is
A floor-standing panoramic X-ray unit in its own room, which images all your teeth and both jaws in one pass.
What it means for you
Routine X-rays are taken here during your visit, with no trip to a separate imaging centre.

Used in: Dental implants, Full mouth rehabilitation, Maxillofacial prosthetics, Root canal treatment, Orthodontics (braces), Invisalign clear aligners, Crowns & bridges, General dentistry & cleaning, Kids’ dentistry

Plan precisely

Digital planning

Planning on screen

What it is
A consultation desk in the treatment room, with a large monitor for X-rays and photographs.
What it means for you
Your options are talked through on screen, at a desk, before anything is decided.

Used in: Smile design, Dental implants, Full mouth rehabilitation, Invisalign clear aligners, Cosmetic dentistry, Maxillofacial prosthetics, Orthodontics (braces)

Treat gently

Comfort and precision

Treatment rooms

What it is
Rooms with a fully adjustable dental chair, an overhead operating light and a screen beside the chair.
What it means for you
You lie back comfortably, the work area is well lit, and your images can be shown to you as we explain.

Used in: General dentistry & cleaning, Root canal treatment, Crowns & bridges, Cosmetic dentistry, Kids’ dentistry, Orthodontics (braces), Dental implants, Smile design

Rotary endodontics

What it is
Fine, flexible files driven by a motor, used to clean and shape the canals inside a tooth.
What it means for you
The canals are cleaned evenly during root canal treatment.

Used in: Root canal treatment

Kept safe

Sterilisation

Steam sterilisation

What it is
A separate sterilisation room with a benchtop steam steriliser and its own sink.
What it means for you
Instruments are cleaned and sterilised in their own room, away from where you are treated.

Used in every treatment

Instrument care

What it is
An automatic unit that cleans and oils the dental handpieces, beside a water distiller.
What it means for you
Handpieces are maintained after use, and distilled water is made on site for the equipment.

Used in every treatment

Why it matters

Good equipment does not replace judgement. It helps a careful dentist see more, plan more exactly and work more gently, and it keeps every visit clean and safe.

Suitability for any treatment is decided at your consultation.
