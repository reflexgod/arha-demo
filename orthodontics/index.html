Orthodontics (braces)

Braces and aligners for children, teens and adults.

Book a consultationCall now

Straight teeth at any age

Orthodontics moves teeth into a better position, for a bite that works well and teeth that are easier to clean.

Metal braces and clear aligners are both available at Arha, for children, teens and adults, with an early orthodontic assessment for children.

Is this for you?

Crowded or crooked teeth
Teeth that overlap, turn or sit out of line.
Gaps
Spaces between teeth.
Teeth that stick out
Front teeth that are prominent or easily knocked.
A bite that does not meet properly
Teeth that do not come together evenly.

Often chosen for

  • Crooked teeth
  • My child’s teeth

How it works

Step by step, from the first conversation to the care that follows.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    An examination of the teeth, bite and, for children, how the jaws are growing.

  2. 02

    Records and plan

    Photographs and X-rays, then a plan with the options that suit you.

  3. 03

    Braces or aligners fitted

    Your braces are fitted, or your first aligners given.

  4. 04

    Regular reviews

    Adjustments and checks along the way.

  5. 05

    Retainers

    Retainers keep the teeth in their new position.

What usually suits each age

Hover or tap an age group to see what is usually suitable.

  • Child (7 to 11)

    An early orthodontic check around age 7 spots problems with how the jaws and teeth are growing. Most children only need monitoring; some benefit from early treatment.

    Invisalign clear aligners

  • Teen (12 to 18)

    Most adult teeth are in, so this is the most common time for braces, or aligners where they suit.

    Invisalign clear aligners

  • Adult

    It is never too late. Many adults choose clear aligners; braces suit some bites better. Gum health is checked first.

    Invisalign clear aligners

Technology & innovation

Technology used

  • Panoramic X-rayRoutine X-rays are taken here during your visit, with no trip to a separate imaging centre.
  • Treatment roomsYou lie back comfortably, the work area is well lit, and your images can be shown to you as we explain.
  • Planning on screenYour options are talked through on screen, at a desk, before anything is decided.
  • SterilisationInstruments are cleaned and sterilised in their own room, away from where you are treated.
