Dental tourism

Planning dental treatment in Mumbai from abroad: one virtual consultation, a written plan, then your visits.

Why Arha

  • On Peddar Road, South Mumbai

    Opposite Jaslok Hospital, in Doctor House, 14 Peddar Road.

  • Close to Marine Drive

    South Mumbai has a wide choice of hotels and serviced apartments a short drive away.

  • Specialist-led

    Implants, full mouth rehabilitation and smile design, led by a prosthodontist.

  • Planned before you fly

    Your plan, visits and written estimate are agreed before you book travel.

How it works

  1. Step 01

    Virtual consultation

    By phone or video call: your concerns, your photographs and any recent X-rays.

  2. Step 02

    Plan and written estimate

    A treatment plan with the number of visits, how long each takes, and the costs.

  3. Step 03

    Arrival

    Your first appointment is fixed before you land.

  4. Step 04

    Treatment visits

    Planned around the time you have in Mumbai.

  5. Step 05

    Remote follow-up

    Reviews by phone or video call once you are home.

Questions, answered
