Book an appointment

Choose an in-person visit at Peddar Road or a virtual consultation from anywhere. What happens on your first visit.

At the clinic, Peddar Road

In-person visit

A full examination and a clear explanation of your options, in person, with time for every question.

From anywhere

Virtual consultation

A video consultation to talk through your concerns, look at any X-rays or scans you already have, and plan your treatment before you travel.

Riya, our online receptionist, takes your name, what you need and a time that suits you, and the clinic confirms your appointment.

Your first visit

Five steps, at your pace. Nothing begins until you have heard your options and decided.

Arrive at Doctor House

Arha is at 107, Doctor House, 14 Peddar Road, opposite Jaslok Hospital. You are welcomed at reception and wait in the patients’ lounge.

Talk with the doctor

You start by talking, not in the chair: what you have noticed, how long it has been going on, your health and medicines, and what you would like.

A careful check

An examination of your teeth and gums. If an X-ray is needed, it is taken in the clinic’s own X-ray room, so you don’t have to go anywhere else.

Your plan, explained

The doctor shows you what they found and explains your options in plain words, before anything begins. Ask anything; there is time for every question.

Next steps

You decide in your own time. If you would like to go ahead, the clinic arranges your next appointment with you.

What to bring

  • Any recent dental X-rays or reports you already have
  • A list of the medicines you take
  • Your questions; it helps to write them down
  • For a child or an older parent: someone they trust can come along
