What it usually means

Finding the cause of the pain

Pain when you bite, or with hot and cold, often comes from decay, a crack or an inflamed nerve. An examination and an X-ray show which. Severe pain or swelling should be seen soon.

Go to a hospital first if swelling spreads towards your eye or neck, or if breathing or swallowing is hard.

Repairing a chipped tooth

A small chip can often be rebuilt in tooth-coloured bonding. A larger break may need a crown, a cap that protects the tooth. If it hurts, it should be seen soon.
