Common problems we fix

A gap changes how you chew, and over time how the teeth beside it sit. It can be replaced with an implant, a bridge or a denture.

See how we fix it

A small chip can often be rebuilt in tooth-coloured bonding. A larger break may need a crown to protect what is left.

See how we fix it

Gaps can be closed by adding to the teeth with bonding or veneers, or by moving them with aligners. The right choice depends on the gap and your bite.

See how we fix it

Teeth can be straightened at almost any age, with clear aligners or with braces. Straighter teeth are also easier to keep clean.

See how we fix it

Surface stains often lift with a professional clean. Deeper colour responds to whitening, and where whitening cannot help, bonding or veneers can.

See how we fix it

Sensitivity can come from worn enamel or receding gums; pain that lingers often means decay has reached the nerve. An exam and an X-ray tell which it is.

See how we fix it

Gums that bleed when you brush are usually inflamed, and that is treatable. A professional clean and better care at home often settle it.

See how we fix it

Old metal fillings can crack, or leak at the edges, over the years. When one needs replacing, it can be redone in tooth-coloured material, or with a crown.

See how we fix it

A denture that moves when you eat or speak can be held firmly by a few implants. Sometimes a refit or a new denture is enough.

See how we fix it

Baby teeth hold space for the adult teeth and matter for eating and speech. Regular checks, sealants and fluoride help stop decay early.

See how we fix it

Technology & innovation

Equipped in-house

Three things you will find in Arha’s own rooms. Tap a number to see what it is, and what it means for you.

01

An in-house panoramic X-ray

What it is
A panoramic X-ray unit in a room of its own at the clinic, showing all the teeth and both jaws in one image.
What it means for you
Your X-rays are taken during your visit, so your plan can be explained the same day, with no trip to an imaging centre.

02

Digital 3D models of your teeth

What it is
Scans of your teeth are viewed on screen in the treatment room as 3D models.
What it means for you
You can see your own teeth from every side, and how a plan would change them, before any treatment starts.

03

A sterilisation room of its own

What it is
Instruments are cleaned and sterilised in a separate room, in a steam steriliser, away from where you are treated.
What it means for you
Everything used for your treatment has been through the same careful routine, every time.
