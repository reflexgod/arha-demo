Ten common problems

Choose the one closest to yours. Nothing here is a diagnosis: Dr. Nikhil confirms what is going on, and your options, at your visit.

  • A missing tooth A gap changes how you chew, and over time how the teeth beside it sit. It can be replaced with an implant, a bridge or a denture.
  • Chipped or broken tooth A small chip can often be rebuilt in tooth-coloured bonding. A larger break may need a crown to protect what is left.Usually: Bonding, a crown or a veneer
  • Gaps between teeth Gaps can be closed by adding to the teeth with bonding or veneers, or by moving them with aligners. The right choice depends on the gap and your bite.
  • Crooked teeth Teeth can be straightened at almost any age, with clear aligners or with braces. Straighter teeth are also easier to keep clean.Usually: Clear aligners, braces or smile design
  • Yellow or stained teeth Surface stains often lift with a professional clean. Deeper colour responds to whitening, and where whitening cannot help, bonding or veneers can.
  • Toothache or sensitivity Sensitivity can come from worn enamel or receding gums; pain that lingers often means decay has reached the nerve. An exam and an X-ray tell which it is.Usually: A filling, root canal treatment or a crown
  • Bleeding gums Gums that bleed when you brush are usually inflamed, and that is treatable. A professional clean and better care at home often settle it.Usually: A professional clean, gum care or care at home
  • Old metal fillings Old metal fillings can crack, or leak at the edges, over the years. When one needs replacing, it can be redone in tooth-coloured material, or with a crown.Usually: A tooth-coloured filling, a crown or onlay or watching it
  • Loose dentures A denture that moves when you eat or speak can be held firmly by a few implants. Sometimes a refit or a new denture is enough.Usually: A refit or new denture, implant-retained denture or fixed implant teeth
  • My child’s teeth Baby teeth hold space for the adult teeth and matter for eating and speech. Regular checks, sealants and fluoride help stop decay early.Usually: Check-ups, sealants and fluoride or an early orthodontic check
