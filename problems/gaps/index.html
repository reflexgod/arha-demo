What we fix

Gaps between teeth

Gaps can be closed by adding to the teeth with bonding or veneers, or by moving them with aligners. The right choice depends on the gap and your bite.

Book a consultationCall now

What you may notice

  • A space you see in photos

    Most often between the two front teeth.

  • Food catching

    Food collects in the space when you eat.

  • Gaps that grow

    A gap that widens with time can mean the gums or bite need checking.

  • Speech

    Some people notice a slight whistle on certain sounds.

Three ways to close a gap

Hover or tap each one to see how it works.

  • Bonding

    Tooth-coloured material added to the sides of the teeth. Quick and conservative, for small gaps.

    Cosmetic dentistry

  • Veneers

    Thin porcelain facings that reshape the front teeth, closing gaps while improving shape and colour.

    Cosmetic dentistry

  • Aligners

    Clear trays that move the teeth together. Nothing is added to the teeth.

    Invisalign clear aligners

How we usually fix it

The options, side by side. Which suits you is decided together, after an examination.

  • Bonding

    Tooth-coloured material added to the edges of the teeth to close a small gap.

    Cosmetic dentistry

  • Veneers

    Thin porcelain facings that change the width and shape of the front teeth.

    Cosmetic dentistry

  • Aligners

    Clear trays that move the teeth together, without adding to them.

    Invisalign clear aligners

What to expect

Visits and timing are decided at your consultation.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    You tell us what is bothering you and what you would like. We listen before we look.

  2. 02

    An examination

    A careful look at the teeth, gums and bite, with X-rays if they are needed.

  3. 03

    Your options, explained

    What each option involves, how it would look and feel, and what it costs, so you can decide calmly.

  4. 04

    A plan made for you

    The number of visits and the timing are agreed with you at the consultation, never guessed in advance.
