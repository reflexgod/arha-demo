What we fix

Yellow or stained teeth

Surface stains often lift with a professional clean. Deeper colour responds to whitening, and where whitening cannot help, bonding or veneers can.

Book a consultationCall now

What you may notice

  • Surface stains

    From tea, coffee, paan or smoking, often at the edges and between teeth.

  • An even yellow tone

    Teeth naturally darken a little with age.

  • One darker tooth

    A single tooth that has changed colour should be checked, as it can follow an old knock.

  • White or brown patches

    Spots in the enamel that whitening alone may not change.

How we usually fix it

What to expect

Visits and timing are decided at your consultation.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    You tell us what is bothering you and what you would like. We listen before we look.

  2. 02

    An examination

    A careful look at the teeth, gums and bite, with X-rays if they are needed.

  3. 03

    Your options, explained

    What each option involves, how it would look and feel, and what it costs, so you can decide calmly.

  4. 04

    A plan made for you

    The number of visits and the timing are agreed with you at the consultation, never guessed in advance.
