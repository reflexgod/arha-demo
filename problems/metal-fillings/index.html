What we fix

Old metal fillings

Old metal fillings can crack, or leak at the edges, over the years. When one needs replacing, it can be redone in tooth-coloured material, or with a crown.

Book a consultationCall now

What you may notice

  • A dark grey tooth

    Metal can show through the enamel around it.

  • Cracks around a filling

    Fine lines in the tooth next to an old filling.

  • Sensitivity

    A twinge with cold, or on biting, near the filling.

  • A rough edge

    A filling that feels worn or catches the floss.

Metal or tooth-coloured?

Switch between the two to compare.

Metal

How it looks
Dark grey; it can show through the tooth.
How it is held
Packed into a shaped cavity.
When we replace one
When it is cracked, leaking or has decay beneath it.

Tooth-coloured

How it looks
Matched to the shade of the tooth.
How it is held
Bonded to the tooth, so less of it is drilled away.
When we replace one
It is placed when an old filling needs replacing.

The options, side by side. Which suits you is decided together, after an examination.

What to expect

Visits and timing are decided at your consultation.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    You tell us what is bothering you and what you would like. We listen before we look.

  2. 02

    An examination

    A careful look at the teeth, gums and bite, with X-rays if they are needed.

  3. 03

    Your options, explained

    What each option involves, how it would look and feel, and what it costs, so you can decide calmly.

  4. 04

    A plan made for you

    The number of visits and the timing are agreed with you at the consultation, never guessed in advance.
