What we fix

My child’s teeth

Baby teeth hold space for the adult teeth and matter for eating and speech. Regular checks, sealants and fluoride help stop decay early.

Book a consultationCall now

What you may notice

  • The first teeth

    Usually from around six months; the first visit can follow soon after.

  • Marks or spots

    White, brown or dark patches on the teeth.

  • Crowding

    Adult teeth coming in crooked or behind the baby teeth.

  • Habits

    Thumb-sucking or a dummy beyond the early years.

What to expect at each age

Slide to your child’s age.

061218

Birth to 2

The first teeth arrive. Book the first visit around the first tooth or the first birthday, and wipe or brush the teeth twice a day.

3 to 5 years

All the baby teeth are in. Short check-ups, fluoride and help with brushing; watch for sweet drinks.

6 to 9 years

The first adult molars come through. Sealants protect their grooves; an early orthodontic check spots crowding.

10 to 12 years

The last baby teeth go. A good time to plan braces or aligners if the bite needs it.

Teenagers

Adult teeth in place. Regular cleans, sports mouthguards, and straightening if wanted.

What to expect

Visits and timing are decided at your consultation.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    You tell us what is bothering you and what you would like. We listen before we look.

  2. 02

    An examination

    A careful look at the teeth, gums and bite, with X-rays if they are needed.

  3. 03

    Your options, explained

    What each option involves, how it would look and feel, and what it costs, so you can decide calmly.

  4. 04

    A plan made for you

    The number of visits and the timing are agreed with you at the consultation, never guessed in advance.
