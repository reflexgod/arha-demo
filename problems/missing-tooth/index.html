What we treat

A missing tooth

A gap changes how you chew, and over time how the teeth beside it sit. It can be replaced with an implant, a bridge or a denture.

Book an appointmentCall now

What you may notice

  • Chewing on one side

    You avoid the gap without thinking about it.

  • Teeth shifting

    The teeth next to the gap, and the one opposite, can drift into the space.

  • Food trapping

    Food collects in the gap and at the edges of the teeth beside it.

  • A change in your smile

    A gap near the front shows when you talk or smile.

Implant, bridge or denture?

Choose an option to see it, and how it compares.

Implant

Fixed or removable
Fixed. It stays in, like a natural tooth.
The teeth beside the gap
Left untouched.
How long it lasts
Many years with good care and regular checks.

Bridge

Fixed or removable
Fixed. It is cemented in place.
The teeth beside the gap
Shaped to hold the crowns that support the bridge.
How long it lasts
Many years with good care; the supporting teeth must stay healthy.

Denture

Fixed or removable
Removable. You take it out to clean it.
The teeth beside the gap
Untouched, or used for small clasps.
How long it lasts
Several years; it is refitted or remade as the gums change.

General information. Which option suits you depends on your gums, your bone and the teeth around the gap.

How we usually fix it

The options, side by side. Which suits you is decided together, after an examination.

  • An implant

    A fixed tooth on a small titanium post in the jaw. The teeth beside it are left as they are.

    Dental implants

  • A bridge

    A fixed tooth held by crowns on the teeth either side of the gap.

    Crowns & bridges

  • A denture

    A removable tooth or teeth on a base that rests on the gums.

    Full mouth rehabilitation

What to expect

Visits and timing are decided at your consultation.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    You tell us what is bothering you and what you would like. We listen before we look.

  2. 02

    An examination

    A careful look at the teeth, gums and bite, with X-rays if they are needed.

  3. 03

    Your options, explained

    What each option involves, how it would look and feel, and what it costs, so you can decide calmly.

  4. 04

    A plan made for you

    The number of visits and the timing are agreed with you at the consultation, never guessed in advance.
