What we treat

Bleeding gums

Gums that bleed when you brush are usually inflamed, and that is treatable. A professional clean and better care at home often settle it.

Book an appointmentCall now

What you may notice

  • Pink in the sink

    Bleeding when you brush or floss.

  • Red or puffy gums

    Healthy gums are pale pink and firm.

  • Bad breath

    Breath that does not freshen after brushing.

  • Gums pulling back

    Teeth that look longer, or feel loose.

A gum self-check

Three questions you can answer at home.

01Do your gums bleed when you brush or floss?
02Are your gums red, puffy or tender?
03Do you notice bad breath, or gums pulling back from the teeth?

Answer the three questions to see your result.

Your gums sound healthy

Keep brushing twice a day and cleaning between the teeth, and come for your regular check-up and clean.

What to expect

Visits and timing are decided at your consultation.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    You tell us what is bothering you and what you would like. We listen before we look.

  2. 02

    An examination

    A careful look at the teeth, gums and bite, with X-rays if they are needed.

  3. 03

    Your options, explained

    What each option involves, how it would look and feel, and what it costs, so you can decide calmly.

  4. 04

    A plan made for you

    The number of visits and the timing are agreed with you at the consultation, never guessed in advance.
