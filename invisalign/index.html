Invisalign clear aligners

Straightening teeth with a series of clear, removable aligners.

Book an appointmentCall now

What it is

Straighter teeth, almost unseen

Clear aligners are thin, clear trays made for your teeth. Each set moves them a little, and you change to the next set on a schedule.

Arha offers Invisalign, one brand of clear aligner. Aligners suit many adults and teens, but not every bite; your consultation will tell you whether they suit you, or whether braces would do better.

Is this for you?

Crowded or crooked teeth
Teeth that overlap or turn.
Gaps
Spaces that can be closed by moving the teeth.
Teeth that moved after braces
Relapse after earlier orthodontic treatment.
Adults who prefer not to wear braces
Treatment that fits work and social life.

Often chosen for

  • Gaps between teeth
  • Crooked teeth

What it feels like
Each new set can feel tight for a day or two as the teeth move. Most people find it easy to manage.

Your treatment

How it works

Step by step, from the first conversation to the care that follows.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    An examination to see whether aligners suit your teeth and bite.

  2. 02

    Records and plan

    Photographs, X-rays and impressions or a scan, then a digital plan of every stage.

  3. 03

    Aligners fitted

    You receive your first aligners and learn how to wear and clean them.

  4. 04

    Reviews

    Short visits to check progress and give you the next sets.

  5. 05

    Retainers

    Retainers hold the new position once treatment ends.

How aligners move teeth

Step through treatment from scan to straighter teeth, then compare aligners with braces.

Stage 1Stage 2Stage 3Stage 4

Your scan

Your teeth are recorded with photographs, X-rays and a scan or impressions, and every stage is planned on screen.

Your aligners

A series of thin, clear aligners is made for your teeth, each a small step from the last.

Wearing them

You wear each set most of the day and night, and move to the next set when we say.

Straighter teeth

When the last aligner is done, a retainer keeps the teeth in their new place.
