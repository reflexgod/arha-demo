Crowns & bridges

Restoring broken teeth and replacing missing ones, made to match your smile.

Book a consultationCall now

Strength and a natural look

A crown covers and protects a damaged tooth. A bridge replaces a missing tooth by holding a new tooth between two crowns.

Arha offers ceramic, zirconia and PFM (porcelain fused to metal) crowns. The right one depends on where the tooth is and the work it has to do.

Is this for you?

A broken or cracked tooth
A tooth too damaged for a filling.
A large old filling
A tooth weakened by a big filling.
A tooth after root canal treatment
Protection for a more brittle tooth.
One missing tooth
A bridge across the gap, or an implant crown.

How it works

Step by step, from the first conversation to the care that follows.

  1. 01

    Consultation and X-rays

    We check the tooth, its root and your bite.

  2. 02

    Preparing the tooth

    The tooth is shaped under local anaesthetic so the crown fits.

  3. 03

    Temporary crown

    A temporary protects the tooth while the crown is made.

  4. 04

    Fitting

    The crown or bridge is fitted and the bite adjusted.

  5. 05

    Review

    A check that it feels right and your gums are healthy.

Technology & innovation

Technology used

  • Panoramic X-rayRoutine X-rays are taken here during your visit, with no trip to a separate imaging centre.
  • Treatment roomsYou lie back comfortably, the work area is well lit, and your images can be shown to you as we explain.
