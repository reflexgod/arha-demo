Crowns & bridges

Restoring broken teeth and replacing missing ones, made to match your smile.

Book a Consultation Call +91 00000 00000

Strength and a natural look

A crown covers and protects a damaged tooth. A bridge replaces a missing tooth by holding a new tooth between two crowns.

Arha offers ceramic, zirconia and PFM (porcelain fused to metal) crowns. The right one depends on where the tooth is and the work it has to do.

Is this for you?

  • A broken or cracked tooth A tooth too damaged for a filling.
  • A large old filling A tooth weakened by a big filling.
  • A tooth after root canal treatment Protection for a more brittle tooth.
  • One missing tooth A bridge across the gap, or an implant crown.

How it works

  1. Step 01

    Consultation and X-rays

    We check the tooth, its root and your bite.

  2. Step 02

    Preparing the tooth

    The tooth is shaped under local anaesthetic so the crown fits.

  3. Step 03

    Temporary crown

    A temporary protects the tooth while the crown is made.

  4. Step 04

    Fitting

    The crown or bridge is fitted and the bite adjusted.

  5. Step 05

    Review

    A check that it feels right and your gums are healthy.

Questions, answered

Crowns are long-term restorations. Care at home, habits such as grinding and regular check-ups all affect how long they last.

It is done under local anaesthetic, so you should feel pressure rather than pain.

There is no single best one. It depends on the tooth, your bite and how it needs to look; we will recommend one and explain why.

An implant leaves the neighbouring teeth untouched; a bridge relies on them. The decider above gives the general idea, and your consultation the answer for you.
