Crowns, bridges and full-mouth rehabilitation

When several teeth are worn, broken or missing, repairing them one by one rarely lasts. Full-mouth rehabilitation restores your bite as a whole.

We plan it in stages, with trial teeth where needed, so you can see and feel the result before it is final.

Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment removes infection from inside a tooth so you can keep it.

We use local anaesthetic and take the time each step needs. Afterwards, we protect the tooth, usually with a crown.
