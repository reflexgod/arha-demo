What does a prosthodontist do?

Prosthodontics is the dental specialty concerned with restoring and replacing teeth: crowns, bridges, dentures and the teeth that sit on dental implants. A prosthodontist's training covers how the teeth, the bite and the jaw work together, which matters most when several teeth need treatment at once.

That is why implant and full-mouth treatment at Arha starts from the final teeth. Dr. Nikhil plans how they should look, bite and be cleaned, and the treatment is planned to support them.
