Smile design

Your new smile, planned around your face and previewed before any work begins.

Book a Consultation Call +91 00000 00000

Planned first, then made

Smile design is the planning that comes before cosmetic treatment: the length, width, shape and colour of your teeth, considered with your lips, your face and your bite.

You see and approve the plan before any tooth is touched. The treatment that follows may be whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns or aligners, often a mix.

How a smile is planned

Everything is measured, planned and previewed before work starts. Turn on the design guides to see what we look at.

Measured before anything is changed

The midline between the front teeth is matched to the centre of your face. The smile line along the biting edges follows your lower lip. Each tooth’s proportions are planned in relation to its neighbours.

The design is then previewed so you can see it, and change it, before treatment.

Before and after the design

Uneven lengths and widths brought into proportion along the smile line.

Is this for you?

  • Teeth of uneven length or shape

    Edges that do not follow your lip line, or teeth out of proportion.

  • Gaps, chips or worn edges

    Small flaws that add up across a smile.

  • A smile that shows too much or too little

    Tooth length and gum line considered together.

  • Old cosmetic work you are unhappy with

    Crowns or veneers that no longer look right.

How it works

  1. Step 01

    Consultation and photographs

    What you like and dislike about your smile, with photographs and records.

  2. Step 02

    Smile analysis and design

    Proportions, midline and smile line, planned around your face.

  3. Step 03

    Preview

    A mock-up lets you see the planned shape before anything is changed.

  4. Step 04

    Treatment

    The planned work is carried out, often in stages.

  5. Step 05

    Review

    We check the result, your bite and your comfort.
