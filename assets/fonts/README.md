# Fonts

Self-hosted WOFF2 (latin) from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence texts beside them).

- `cormorant-garamond-500-600.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond, variable weight (used at 500 and 600): headings, large quotes, the doctors' names.
- `cormorant-garamond-italic-500.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond Italic 500: the hero's one accent word only.
- `jost-400.woff2`, `jost-500.woff2`: Jost 400 (body, forms, footer) and 500 (nav, buttons, small uppercase lines).

Preloaded on every page: Cormorant Garamond 500 and Jost 400. Fallbacks are metric-matched in site.css (section 1).
