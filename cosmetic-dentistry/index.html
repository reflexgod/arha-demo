Cosmetic dentistry

Veneers, whitening and bonding, chosen for what your teeth actually need.

Book a Consultation Call +91 00000 00000

The lightest treatment that does the job

Cosmetic dentistry improves the colour, shape and alignment of teeth. The best result often comes from the least invasive option.

Every option is explained at your consultation: what it involves, how it is looked after, and how long it can be expected to last.

Is this for you?

  • Yellow or stained teeth General yellowing, or stains that brushing does not shift.
  • Small chips and worn edges Edges that have chipped or worn unevenly.
  • Small gaps Spaces between front teeth.
  • Teeth of uneven shape Teeth that are short, narrow or out of proportion.

Which fix for which concern

Choose a concern to see which treatments usually suit it, and why.

Stains

Whitening

A good fit

Lightens natural tooth colour; works best on general yellowing.

Bonding

Sometimes

Can cover a dark patch on a single tooth.

Veneers

Sometimes

For deep stains that whitening cannot lift.

Chips

Whitening

Not for this

Changes colour, not shape.

Bonding

A good fit

Tooth-coloured resin rebuilds small chips with little or no drilling.

Veneers

Sometimes

For larger chips, or when several teeth need reshaping.

Gaps

Whitening

Not for this

Changes colour, not spacing.

Bonding

A good fit

Adds width to close small gaps.

Veneers

Sometimes

Closes gaps while also changing shape and colour.

Shape

Whitening

Not for this

Changes colour, not shape.

Bonding

Sometimes

Small changes to length or edges.

Veneers

A good fit

Changes the shape, length and colour of several teeth together.

Large gaps or crooked teeth may be better treated by moving them. See Invisalign clear aligners.

Questions, answered

Professional whitening is planned after an examination, with the strength and timing suited to your teeth. Some sensitivity for a short time is common.

Veneers are a long-term treatment that depends on care, habits such as grinding, and regular reviews. We will explain what to expect for you.

Bonding can pick up stains over time, more than porcelain. Polishing at check-ups helps keep it looking fresh.

Usually a thin layer of enamel is shaped so the veneer sits naturally. How much depends on your plan; some cases need very little.
