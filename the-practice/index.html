About

The practice

Doctor House, 14 Peddar Road: reception, lounge, treatment rooms and the rooms behind them.

Book an appointmentCall now

A clinic built around calm

Marble, soft light and quiet rooms. Arha is on the first floor of Doctor House, opposite Jaslok Hospital, and every room was planned so that specialist dentistry feels unhurried.

You arrive at a reception and a lounge, away from the treatment rooms. X-rays are taken in their own room, and instruments are cleaned and sterilised in a separate room of their own.

Hygiene & sterilisation

Kept safe, in a room of its own

Instruments are cleaned and sterilised in a separate room with a benchtop steam steriliser, away from where you are treated. Handpieces are cleaned and oiled by an automatic unit after use.
