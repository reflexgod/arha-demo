### Doctor photos: how to swap them in (one file)
The placeholders (gold logo on stone) stay until the photos arrive. To swap in a doctor's photo, add up to two lines to
`content/media.yaml` (`images:` list), then run `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/v5_media.py images <slot names>` and build:

```yaml
  - {name: doctor-dr-nikhil-jain-card, kind: real, src: src-assets/doctors/nikhil-landscape.jpg, ratio: [400, 310], widths: [480, 800], focus: [0.5, 0.3], alt: "Dr. Nikhil V. Jain"}
  - {name: doctor-dr-nikhil-jain-portrait, kind: real, src: src-assets/doctors/nikhil-portrait.jpg, ratio: [720, 740], widths: [480, 800, 1440], focus: [0.5, 0.35], pos: "50% 25%", alt: "Dr. Nikhil V. Jain"}
```

- `-card` = the team cards (home, the practice, "Also at Arha" on the other doctor's profile). `-portrait` = the
  profile-page hero. Either one can be added alone; anything missing keeps its placeholder.
- Slot names come from the doctor's `id` in content/site.yaml: `dr-nikhil-jain`, `dr-vasant-jain`.
- Taking the line out again (and rerunning v5_media.py) brings the placeholder back.
- Tested 17 Nov with a stand-in photo in all four places at 1440 and 390; the stand-in was removed.

**What to ask the clinic for (per doctor, same session):**
- Two shots: one **portrait** (4:5 or 3:4) and one **landscape** (3:2 or 4:3). The boxes they fill are the profile hero
  (about 1:1 on desktop, 16:9 on phones) and the team card (4:3.1).
- At least **2400 px on the short side**. Straight from the camera or phone (JPEG or HEIC), with no filters, beauty mode or
  portrait blur added.
- Head and shoulders to mid-chest, a hand's width of space above the head, and the doctor centred, so the 16:9 phone crop
  still keeps the whole head.
- Plain light background or the reception marble, with soft natural window light (no flash). Clinic coat or what they
  wear with patients.
- Both doctors shot in the same place and light, so the cards match.

  Turnstile secret stays only in Apps Script properties.

## Commands
- Build: `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/build.py`. It checks titles ≤60, descriptions ≤155, one H1, alt text, dead
  links, banned words and raw TBCs.
- JSON-LD: `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/qa/jsonld_check.py <schemaorg-current-https.jsonld>` (download the vocabulary
  from schema.org first).
- Images/video: `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/v5_media.py images|videos [names]`
- Stock: `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/v5_stock.py search|pick …` (Unsplash, 50 calls/hour)
- TBC list: `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/tbc_list.py`
- Mobile QA: `.venv/Scripts/python -I tools/qa_mobile.py 390` (and 360)
- Lighthouse: serve dist with `tools/qa/gzserve.py 8090 dist`, then `sh tools/qa/lh.sh <out> http://localhost:8090/ 3`
- Git Bash: prefix commands that pass URL paths like `/blog/` with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`.
