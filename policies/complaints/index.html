Complaints policy

How to raise a concern or complaint

Arha Dental Care: last updated 12 November 2026

We want every patient to be well looked after. If something has gone wrong, or you're unhappy with any part of your care or our service, please tell us. We take every concern seriously and we would rather hear about it than not.

This policy explains how to raise a complaint, how we will handle it, and what you can do if you are not satisfied with our response. [[TBC: policy reviewed by the clinic and its legal adviser]]

Our commitment to you

We will treat your complaint with respect and in confidence, look into it fairly, and reply as quickly as we can. Raising a concern will never affect the care you receive from us.

How to make a complaint

You can raise a concern in whichever way suits you:

In person: speak to our reception team or ask to speak to the dentist in charge.

By phone: call us on 99755 07714 during opening hours.

In writing: Arha Dental Care, 107, Doctor House, 14 Peddar Road (Dr Gopalrao Deshmukh Marg), Mumbai 400026.

By email: [[TBC: clinic email]]the enquiry form on our contact page

Please include your name, contact details and a description of your concern, with any dates and the treatment involved. If you are complaining on behalf of another patient, please confirm that they have agreed.

Time limits

Please raise a complaint as soon as you can, ideally within 12 months of the event or of becoming aware of it. We will still consider a later complaint where it is possible to look into it fairly. [[TBC: time limit the clinic wants to state]]

What happens after you complain

We will acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and aim to send you a full reply within 20 working days. If we need longer, we will tell you why and keep you updated. [[TBC: response times]]

Our reply will explain what we found, what we have done or will do about it, and any changes we will make. We are always happy to meet you to talk it through.

If you are not satisfied

If you are not happy with our response, you can take your concern further:

Maharashtra State Dental Council: for concerns about the professional conduct of a dentist registered in Maharashtra. [[TBC: council contact details]]

Dental Council of India: the national body for dental education and professional standards. dciindia.gov.in [[TBC: check whether the National Dental Commission now handles these concerns]]

Consumer commissions: under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, you can contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915, consumerhelpline.gov.in) or file a complaint with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

[[TBC: wording of this section checked by the clinic's legal adviser]]

Learning from complaints

We review every complaint and its outcome, and use what we learn to improve our care and our service.

Confidentiality

Complaints are handled in confidence. Information about your complaint is shared only with the people who need it to look into and resolve it, or where the law requires it. See our privacy policy.
