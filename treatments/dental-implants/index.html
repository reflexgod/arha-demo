Dental implants in South Mumbai, planned by a prosthodontist

Prosthodontist-led dental implant care in South Mumbai, with every step explained before it begins.

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth, so the new tooth is held firmly in the jaw instead of resting on the gum or relying on the teeth beside it. A single implant can carry one crown, two or more can carry a bridge, and a few can hold a full denture in place. Because Dr. Nikhil V. Jain is a prosthodontist, a specialist in replacing and restoring teeth, your treatment starts with the tooth you will see and use, and the implant is planned around it. For anyone missing one tooth or several, implants are often the closest thing to having your own teeth back.

about

What are dental implants?

A dental implant has three parts: the implant itself, a small titanium post that sits in the jawbone where the root used to be; an abutment, a connector at the gum line; and the crown, bridge or denture you see.

Over the healing period, the bone bonds with the implant's surface. Once it has settled, the final tooth is fixed on top and works much like a natural one when you bite and chew.

benefits

Why we recommend dental implants
  • The healthy teeth on either side are left untouched, unlike a conventional bridge.
  • An implant tooth is held in the jaw, so it does not move when you eat or speak.
  • An implant keeps the jawbone working where a tooth is missing, which helps it hold its shape.
  • Implants can turn a loose denture into one that stays firmly in place.
who is it for?

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults who have lost one or more teeth can be considered for implants. Your gum health, the amount of bone in your jaw, and your general health all play a part, as do smoking and conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes.

Before any treatment, we examine your mouth and take the X-rays or scans needed to see the bone. If there is not enough bone, there may still be options, and we explain them at your consultation.

process

Explaining implant treatment

From the first consultation to the final tooth usually takes a few months, because the bone needs time to bond with the implant. Treatment that needs bone grafting takes longer. You receive your own timeline after the consultation. [[TBC: Arha's typical timeline]]

  1. Consultation and planning. Examination, X-rays or a scan, and a written plan with the options and costs.
  2. Placing the implant. A short procedure under local anaesthetic.
  3. Healing. The bone bonds with the implant; we review how it is settling.
  4. The final tooth. Your crown, bridge or denture is made, fitted and checked against your bite.
aftercare

Caring for your dental implants

Clean around an implant as carefully as around your natural teeth: brush twice a day and clean between the teeth with floss or small interdental brushes. Regular check-ups and professional cleaning help protect the gum and bone around the implant, which is what keeps it secure over the years.

implant-related treatments

Implant-retained dentures

What they are

An implant-retained denture is a full denture held in place by a small number of implants, instead of resting on the gum alone. It can be removable for cleaning, or fixed so that only the dentist takes it out.

How they work

The implants are placed in the jaw and allowed to heal. Attachments on the implants then match fittings inside the denture, so it clips firmly into place. The number of implants depends on your jaw and whether the denture is removable or fixed. [[TBC: the implant-retained denture options Arha offers]]

Benefits

  • The denture stays in place when you eat, speak and laugh.
  • Many people find they can eat a wider range of food.
  • An upper denture held by implants may need less coverage of the palate. [[TBC: confirm with Dr. Nikhil V. Jain]]

implant-related treatments

Bone grafting and sinus lift surgery

An implant needs enough healthy bone around it. When the jaw has shrunk after a tooth was lost, the bone can sometimes be built up first. Not everyone needs this: your X-rays or scan show whether you do. [[TBC: whether bone grafting and sinus lifts are done at Arha]]

Bone grafting

Graft material is placed where the jaw is too thin or too low, either before the implant or at the same time. Over the healing period, your own bone grows into it.

Sinus lift

In the upper back jaw, the sinus can sit close to where an implant needs to go. A sinus lift gently raises the lining of the sinus and adds bone beneath it, to make room for the implant.

What it means for your timeline

Grafting usually adds healing time before the final tooth is fitted. We include it in your written plan, so you know the timeline before treatment begins.

faqs

Find answers to popular implant questions below

Does getting an implant hurt?

The area is numbed with local anaesthetic before the implant is placed, so you should feel pressure rather than pain. Some swelling or tenderness for a few days afterwards is common, and we explain how to manage it.

How long do dental implants last?

Implants are a long-term replacement. Like natural teeth, they depend on good cleaning at home and regular reviews, and the crown on top may need attention over the years.

What does a dental implant cost?

The cost depends on how many implants you need, whether bone grafting is required and the kind of tooth placed on top. You receive a written estimate after your consultation. See our fee guide for more.

Can I have an implant if I have lost bone?

Bone loss does not always rule implants out. Once we have seen your X-rays or scan, we explain the options that suit you.
