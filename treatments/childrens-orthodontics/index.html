Orthodontics for children and teenagers in South Mumbai

Crooked, crowded or protruding teeth are common as children grow. An orthodontic check helps you understand whether your child needs treatment, when to start it, and which option suits them. At Arha we offer metal braces and clear aligners for children, teenagers and adults.

Orthodontic care for children in South Mumbai, planned around a growing smile.

Children's teeth and jaws change quickly between the ages of about seven and fifteen. Some problems with the bite are easier to guide while a child is still growing; others are better treated once the adult teeth have come through. We examine your child, take the X-rays needed, and explain what we see in words they understand, so the whole family can decide together. We offer metal braces and clear aligners, including Invisalign®.

about

What is orthodontic treatment for children?

Orthodontic treatment moves teeth, and sometimes guides how the jaws grow, so that the teeth meet properly and are easier to keep clean. For children and teenagers it is usually done with fixed braces or with a series of removable clear aligners. Arha offers both: metal braces and clear aligners.

who is it for?

Who is it suitable for?

An orthodontic check is worth having if you notice any of the following:

  • Crowding: teeth that overlap because there is not enough room.
  • Spacing: gaps between teeth.
  • Overbite or overjet: upper front teeth that sit well in front of the lower teeth.
  • Underbite: lower front teeth that sit in front of the upper teeth.
  • Crossbite: some upper teeth biting inside the lower teeth.
  • Open bite: front teeth that don't meet when the back teeth bite together.

When should my child have a first check?

Many dentists suggest a first orthodontic check at around seven years old, when the first adult teeth are coming through. Most children who need braces start treatment later, often between eleven and fourteen.

benefits

Benefits of treatment at the right age

  • Easier cleaning: straight teeth are easier to brush and clean between.
  • A comfortable bite: teeth that meet properly wear more evenly.
  • Protection: correcting protruding front teeth can make them less likely to be injured.
  • Confidence: many children feel happier about their smile once treatment is complete.

process

What to expect during treatment

  1. Consultation. An examination, X-rays and photographs, and a chat with you and your child.
  2. Your plan. We explain the options, how long each takes and the cost, in writing.
  3. Fitting. Braces are fitted, or the first aligners are given with instructions.
  4. Reviews. Regular visits to check progress and make adjustments. [[TBC: usual review interval]]
  5. Retainers. Once the teeth are in place, retainers hold them there.

timeline

How long does treatment take?

It depends on how much the teeth need to move and how well appliances are worn. Many courses of treatment for teenagers take between one and two years, followed by retainers. You receive your child's own estimate after the consultation. [[TBC: typical durations at Arha]]

faqs

Find answers to popular questions about children's orthodontics

What is the right age for braces?

There is no single right age. A first check at around seven helps spot problems early; most treatment starts once more adult teeth have come through, often between eleven and fourteen.

Do braces hurt?

Teeth often feel tender for a few days after braces are fitted or adjusted, or when a new aligner starts. This usually settles quickly, and we explain how to manage it.

Can my child play sport with braces?

Yes. A mouthguard is recommended for contact sports. Aligners are usually taken out for sport and put back in afterwards.

Will my child need a retainer?

Yes. Teeth tend to drift back after treatment, so retainers are worn afterwards to hold them in place. We explain how long and how often.
