Dental treatments on Peddar Road, planned for lasting results

At Arha Dental Care we look after adults and children, from routine check-ups to complex treatment that rebuilds a whole mouth. The practice is led by Dr. Nikhil V. Jain, a prosthodontist: a dentist with a specialist degree in replacing and restoring teeth.

Every treatment starts with a consultation, an examination and a plan you can read before anything begins. Choose a category below, or book a consultation and we'll talk you through the options.

Questions we are often asked

How do I know which treatment I need?

You don't need to know before you come. At a consultation we examine your teeth, take any X-rays needed and explain the options, with their costs, before anything begins.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see children for check-ups and treatment. Read about our dentistry for children, or call us to book their first visit.

Will I get a written estimate?

We explain the plan and its cost before treatment begins. [[TBC: written estimates as standard]]

Can my dentist refer me to you?

Yes. Dentists can refer patients to Dr. Nikhil V. Jain for implants, crowns, bridges, dentures and full-mouth rehabilitation through our referral form.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us during opening hours. If swelling affects your breathing, swallowing or eye, go to a hospital emergency department straight away.
