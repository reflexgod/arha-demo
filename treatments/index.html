Explore treatments

Specialist care, smile and aesthetic treatment, and everyday care for the whole family.

Book a consultationCall now

Specialist care

Led by a prosthodontist, for the most complex work.

  • Dental implants One tooth, several, or a full arch, planned around the tooth you will see.
  • Full mouth rehabilitation Worn, broken or missing teeth rebuilt as one plan, phase by phase.
  • Maxillofacial prosthetics Custom prostheses after surgery or injury, or for a condition from birth.

Smile & aesthetics

Planned around your face and previewed first.

  • Smile design Your smile planned around your face, and previewed before work begins.
  • Cosmetic dentistry Veneers, whitening and bonding, matched to the concern.
  • Invisalign clear aligners Clear, removable aligners for teens and adults.
  • Orthodontics (braces) Metal braces and clear aligners for children, teens and adults.

Everyday & family care

Check-ups, cleaning and care for the whole family.

  • General dentistry & cleaning Check-ups, professional cleaning and fillings.
  • Root canal treatment Saving an infected tooth, with rotary instruments.
  • Crowns & bridges Ceramic, zirconia and PFM crowns, and bridges for a missing tooth.
  • Kids’ dentistry Gentle first visits, check-ups, sealants and fluoride.
