Root canal treatment in South Mumbai

Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed tissue from inside a tooth, cleans the canals and seals them, so you can keep the tooth. It is done under local anaesthetic, and the tooth is usually protected with a crown afterwards.

Root canal treatment in South Mumbai, done calmly and carefully to save your tooth.

When decay, a crack or an old deep filling lets bacteria reach the nerve inside a tooth, the tissue becomes inflamed or infected. This can cause toothache, sensitivity to hot or cold, or swelling, though sometimes there are no symptoms at all. Root canal treatment cleans out the inside of the tooth and seals it, which usually lets you keep your own tooth instead of having it removed. Because a root-treated tooth is more brittle, we plan how it will be protected, usually with a crown, before treatment begins.

about

What is root canal treatment?

Inside every tooth is soft tissue, the pulp, which runs through narrow channels in the roots called canals. In root canal treatment, the infected or inflamed pulp is removed, the canals are cleaned and shaped, and then they are filled and sealed to stop bacteria getting back in.

benefits

Why we recommend saving the tooth
  • You keep your own tooth, which usually works and feels better than a replacement.
  • Treatment removes the source of infection, which relieves toothache in most cases.
  • It avoids the gap, and the cost of replacing a tooth, that an extraction would leave.
who is it for?

Am I a candidate for root canal treatment?

Signs include toothache that lingers or wakes you at night, pain when biting, sensitivity to hot or cold that lasts after the drink has gone, a darkened tooth, or swelling of the gum. Only an examination and an X-ray can tell whether a tooth needs root canal treatment, or whether a filling is enough. If you have swelling or severe pain, call us the same day.

process

Explaining root canal treatment

Many root canal treatments are finished in one or two visits. Infected or complex teeth can need more, with a dressing placed in the tooth between visits. [[TBC: Arha's usual number of visits]]

  1. Diagnosis. Examination and an X-ray of the tooth.
  2. Cleaning. The tooth is numbed, the pulp removed and the canals cleaned and shaped.
  3. Sealing. The canals are filled and sealed, and the tooth is closed with a filling.
  4. Protecting. Usually a crown, fitted once the tooth has settled.
aftercare

Caring for your tooth afterwards

The tooth may feel tender for a few days, especially when biting; this usually settles on its own. Avoid chewing hard food on that side until the tooth has its permanent filling or crown. Then care for it like your other teeth, with brushing, cleaning between the teeth and regular check-ups.

related treatments

Crowns after root canal treatment

Back teeth in particular are protected with a crown after root canal treatment. Read about crowns and bridges, or, if a tooth cannot be saved, about dental implants.

faqs

Find answers to popular root canal questions below

Is root canal treatment painful?

The tooth is numbed with local anaesthetic before we start, so most people feel pressure rather than pain during treatment. It is often the toothache before treatment that hurts; some tenderness for a few days afterwards is common.

Is a root canal better than an extraction?

Keeping your own tooth is usually the better choice when it can be saved. If it cannot, we explain the ways to replace it.

What does root canal treatment cost?

The cost depends on the tooth (front teeth have fewer canals than molars) and whether a crown is needed. You receive an estimate after the examination.

Can a root canal be redone?

Yes. A tooth whose earlier root canal treatment has failed can often be treated again. We assess it with an X-ray first.
