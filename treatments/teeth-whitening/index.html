Teeth whitening in South Mumbai

Professional teeth whitening lightens the natural colour of your teeth with a whitening gel, applied by the dentist or in custom trays at home. It is safe when supervised by a dentist, but it does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings.

Professional teeth whitening in South Mumbai, with your teeth checked first.

Teeth darken over time with age and with tea, coffee, red wine, turmeric-rich food and smoking. Whitening gels release oxygen into the enamel and lift these stains, making the teeth several shades lighter. Before whitening, we examine your teeth and gums, because decay, gum problems or exposed roots need treating first, and because some discolouration, such as a single dark tooth, responds better to other treatments. about

What is teeth whitening?

The active ingredient, usually hydrogen or carbamide peroxide, breaks down the stain molecules inside the enamel. In-clinic whitening uses a stronger gel with the gums protected; take-home whitening uses a milder gel in thin trays made to fit your teeth, worn for a set time each day.

benefits

Why we recommend professional whitening
  • It lightens your own teeth without drilling or covering them.
  • The gel strength and the trays are matched to your teeth, which helps limit sensitivity.
  • Your teeth and gums are checked first, so problems are not hidden under a brighter shade.
who is it for?

Am I a candidate for teeth whitening?

Whitening suits yellowish teeth stained by age, food and drink. It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for young teenagers, or while there is untreated decay or gum disease. Crowns, veneers and tooth-coloured fillings will not change colour, so they may need replacing to match afterwards.

process

Explaining whitening treatment
  1. Check-up. We examine your teeth and record the starting shade.
  2. Preparation. A clean, and impressions or scans if trays are needed.
  3. Whitening. In the clinic, at home, or both. [[TBC: Arha's method and number of sittings]]
  4. Review. We check the new shade and your gums.
aftercare

Caring for your teeth after whitening

For a day or two after whitening, avoid strongly coloured food and drinks such as tea, coffee, red wine and turmeric-based curries. Brush twice a day and keep up regular cleaning. Some sensitivity is common for a few days and settles on its own; a toothpaste for sensitive teeth can help.

related treatments

When whitening is not enough

Chipped, worn or misshapen teeth, or a single dark tooth, may suit bonding, veneers or a crown instead. Read about smile design.

faqs

Find answers to popular whitening questions below

Is teeth whitening safe?

Whitening with products used or supervised by a dentist is considered safe for the enamel. The most common side effect is temporary sensitivity. Whitening is not advised during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Is teeth whitening permanent?

No. Results usually last from several months to a few years, depending on your diet and habits. Occasional top-ups keep the shade.

Will whitening work on my crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening only lightens natural tooth. Crowns, veneers and fillings stay the same colour and may need replacing to match.

Is whitening safe for my teeth?

Whitening supervised by a dentist, after your teeth and gums have been checked, is a well-established treatment. Some people notice short-lived sensitivity, which usually settles. Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings.
