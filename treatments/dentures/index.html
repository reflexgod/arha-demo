Dentures in Mumbai, made to fit

A denture is a removable set of replacement teeth. Complete dentures replace all the teeth in a jaw; partial dentures fill gaps between your own teeth. At Arha, dentures are planned by Dr. Nikhil V. Jain, a prosthodontist, and can be held more firmly with dental implants.

Dentures in South Mumbai, made by a prosthodontist for your mouth and your bite.

Making a denture is a specialist job: it has to look natural, let you speak and chew, and stay in place. Prosthodontics, Dr. Nikhil V. Jain's specialty, is the branch of dentistry that restores and replaces teeth, and dentures are a core part of that training. We take careful impressions and bite records, let you see and try the teeth before the denture is finished, and adjust it after fitting until it feels right. If an existing denture is loose, we can often reline it, or hold it in place with two or more implants.

about

What are dentures?
  • Complete dentures replace all the teeth in the upper or lower jaw.
  • Partial dentures replace some teeth and clip onto your remaining teeth. They can have a metal framework or be made in a flexible material.
  • Implant-retained dentures click onto two or more dental implants, so they stay firmly in place when you eat and speak.

benefits

Why we recommend dentures
  • You can chew a wider range of food and speak more clearly.
  • Your lips and cheeks are supported, which affects how your face looks.
  • A partial denture stops neighbouring teeth drifting into the gap.
  • Dentures usually cost less than implants or bridges and need no surgery.
who is it for?

Am I a candidate for dentures?

Dentures suit people who have lost several or all of their teeth, people who are not able or ready to have implants, and people who want a removable option. If your current denture is loose, sore or breaking, it may need a reline, a repair or a new denture. We also explain the alternatives, such as implants and bridges, so you can compare them; see replacing missing teeth.

process

Explaining denture treatment
  1. Consultation. We examine your mouth, discuss what you want and plan the denture.
  2. Impressions and bite. Accurate moulds of your gums and records of how your jaws meet.
  3. Try-in. You see and try the teeth set in wax, so the shape, shade and bite can be changed.
  4. Fitting. The finished denture is fitted and adjusted.
  5. Review. Short follow-up visits to ease any sore spots while you get used to it.

aftercare

Caring for your dentures

Clean your dentures every day with a soft brush and denture cleaner, not toothpaste, which can scratch them. Take them out at night unless we advise otherwise, and keep them in water so they don't dry out. Brush your gums and any remaining teeth. Your gums slowly change shape over the years, so come for regular check-ups: a denture that starts to feel loose can often be relined.

related treatments

Holding a denture in place with implants

A lower denture in particular can move when you eat. Two or more implants placed in the jaw can hold it firmly in place, and the denture still comes out for cleaning. Read about dental implants, or about full-mouth rehabilitation if you are considering fixed teeth.

faqs

Find answers to popular denture questions below

How long does it take to get used to new dentures?

Most people need a few weeks to get used to eating and speaking with new dentures. Start with soft food cut into small pieces, and come back if anything feels sore: small adjustments make a big difference.

Can loose dentures be tightened?

Often, yes. A loose denture can usually be relined so that it fits your gums again. If a denture is still loose after relining, implants can hold it in place.

What do dentures cost in Mumbai?

The cost depends on the type of denture, the materials and whether implants are involved. You receive an estimate after your consultation.

Can dentures be fixed permanently?

A standard denture is removable. If you want fixed teeth, a bridge or teeth fixed on implants may be possible. We explain the options at your consultation.
