Smile design in Mumbai

Smile design means planning changes to the shape, colour and position of your teeth so they suit your face and work with your bite. It can combine whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns or aligners, and you see the plan before any treatment begins.

Smile design in South Mumbai, planned around your face and your bite.

People ask about smile design for chipped or worn teeth, gaps, uneven edges, old dark fillings or a colour they don't like. A good result looks natural and lasts, which means it has to fit how your teeth meet as well as how they look. As a prosthodontist, Dr. Nikhil V. Jain plans the shape and bite together, and keeps as much of your own tooth as possible. [[TBC: whether Arha uses digital smile planning or a trial mock-up]]

about

What is smile design?
  • Whitening to lighten the natural colour of your teeth.
  • Composite bonding: tooth-coloured resin added to repair chips and close small gaps, usually without drilling.
  • Veneers: thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of the teeth to change their shape and colour.
  • Crowns for teeth that are heavily filled, broken or root-treated.
  • Aligners to move teeth into a better position first.

benefits

Why we recommend planning the whole smile
  • Each change is chosen to suit your face, lips and gum line, so the result looks like you.
  • Planning the bite helps restorations last and protects them from chipping.
  • Starting with the least invasive option keeps more of your natural teeth.
who is it for?

Am I a candidate for smile design?

It suits adults with healthy gums who would like to change the look of their teeth: chipped, worn, uneven or discoloured teeth, gaps, or old fillings and crowns that no longer match. Decay and gum disease are treated first. If you grind your teeth, we plan for it, as grinding can damage new restorations.

process

Explaining smile design treatment
  1. Consultation. You tell us what you would like to change; we examine your teeth, gums and bite.
  2. Records. Photographs, and impressions or scans.
  3. The plan. We show you the proposed changes, the options and the estimate.
  4. Treatment. Usually from the least to the most involved step, for example whitening before bonding so the new resin matches.
aftercare

Caring for your new smile

Brush twice a day, clean between your teeth, and come for regular check-ups and cleaning. Avoid biting very hard things such as ice or nuts in their shells with bonded or veneered teeth. If you grind your teeth at night, a night guard protects your new smile.

faqs

Find answers to popular smile design questions below

Do veneers damage your teeth?

Porcelain veneers usually need a thin layer of enamel to be removed, which cannot be undone. Composite bonding often needs little or none. We explain how much each option changes your teeth before you decide.

How long do veneers and bonding last?

It varies with your bite, habits and care. Bonding may need polishing or repair over the years; porcelain veneers are more resistant to staining and chipping. We discuss the expected maintenance at your consultation.
