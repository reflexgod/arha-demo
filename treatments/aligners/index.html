Invisalign clear aligners on Peddar Road

Clear aligners are thin, removable plastic trays that move your teeth a little at a time. You change to a new set every week or two, and wear them for most of the day. They suit many adults and teenagers with crowded or gapped teeth.

Clear aligner treatment in South Mumbai, planned after a full examination.

Aligners are planned on a 3D model of your teeth, so you can see the planned movement before treatment starts. Because they come out to eat and to clean your teeth, there are no food restrictions, but they only work if you wear them as prescribed. Arha Dental Care offers Invisalign® clear aligners for adults and teenagers.

about

What are clear aligners?

Each set of aligners is shaped slightly differently from the last. Worn for most of the day, a set applies gentle pressure that moves the teeth towards the next stage. Small tooth-coloured bumps, called attachments, may be added to some teeth to help the aligners grip.

benefits

Why we recommend clear aligners
  • They are clear, so most people won't notice them.
  • They come out for eating, brushing and important occasions.
  • There are no brackets or wires to rub the cheeks.
who is it for?

Am I a candidate for clear aligners?

Aligners can treat many cases of crowding, spacing and some bite problems in adults and teenagers. Very complex bites may need fixed braces or a combination of treatments. Your gums need to be healthy before starting, and you need to wear the aligners for the hours prescribed, usually most of the day and night.

process

Explaining aligner treatment

Treatment time depends on how far the teeth need to move: a few months for minor changes, longer for more complex cases. [[TBC: typical treatment times at Arha]]

  1. Consultation and records. Examination, X-rays, photographs and a 3D scan.
  2. Your plan. You see the planned tooth movement before deciding.
  3. Treatment. New aligners every week or two, with regular check-ins.
  4. Retainers. Worn afterwards to stop the teeth moving back.
aftercare

Caring for your aligners

Take the aligners out to eat and to drink anything other than water. Brush your teeth before putting them back, and rinse the aligners. After treatment, wear your retainers as advised: teeth tend to move back without them.

related treatments

Aligners and smile design

Straightening teeth first often makes whitening or bonding simpler. Read about smile design and teeth whitening. For children and teenagers, see orthodontics for children (metal braces and clear aligners).

faqs

Find answers to popular aligner questions below

Do aligners hurt?

Most people feel pressure or mild soreness for a day or two when they start a new set. This is a sign the aligners are working and usually settles quickly.

Do I need a retainer afterwards?

Yes. Teeth tend to move back after any straightening treatment, so retainers are needed to keep the result.

How many hours a day do I wear aligners?

Most aligner treatment asks you to wear the trays for most of the day and night, taking them out only to eat, to drink anything other than water, and to clean your teeth. Your dentist tells you the wearing time for your plan.
