General dentistry & cleaning

Check-ups, cleaning and fillings that keep problems small.

Book a Consultation Call +91 00000 00000

Prevention first

Regular check-ups find problems while they are still small and simple to treat. A professional clean removes the hard deposits that brushing cannot.

How often you need to come depends on your teeth and gums. We will suggest an interval that suits you.

Is this for you?

  • It has been a while No check-up in the last year or more.
  • Bleeding gums or bad breath Common early signs of gum inflammation.
  • Sensitivity or a small cavity Caught early, often a simple filling.
  • Old fillings that need checking Fillings that are worn, cracked or leaking.

How it works

  1. Step 01

    We ask how you are

    Any pain or sensitivity, and any changes in your health or medicines.

  2. Step 02

    Examination

    Teeth, gums, bite, tongue and the soft tissues of the mouth.

  3. Step 03

    X-rays, if needed

    Taken in-house, and only when they will tell us something useful.

  4. Step 04

    Cleaning

    Scaling and polishing to remove plaque, tartar and surface stains.

  5. Step 05

    Your plan

    What we found, what needs doing now, and what can wait.
