Specialist care

Full mouth rehabilitation

Rebuilding a worn, broken or incomplete set of teeth, and the bite that holds them together.

Book an appointmentCall now

What it is

When the whole bite needs attention

Full mouth rehabilitation brings every tooth, and the way the teeth meet, back into balance. It combines treatments such as crowns, bridges, implants and fillings in one plan.

Because the bite is planned as a whole, each step fits the next. A prosthodontist leads the plan from the first records to the final teeth.

Is this for you?

Teeth worn down by grinding
Short, flat or sensitive teeth that have lost their shape over the years.
Several broken or missing teeth
Gaps and broken teeth that make chewing hard on both sides.
A bite that has collapsed
Teeth that have drifted or shortened so the face looks older and the bite feels wrong.
Old dental work failing in many places
Crowns, bridges and fillings that need replacing together, not one at a time.

Often chosen for

  • A missing tooth
  • Loose dentures

Book an appointmentWhat happens on your first visit

The plan, phase by phase

Each phase has its own purpose. Step through them to see what happens.

01

Step 1 of 5

Diagnose

A full examination, X-rays, photographs and records of how your teeth meet. We start with what bothers you most.

02

Step 2 of 5

Design the bite & smile

Your new bite and tooth shapes are designed on models and on screen, around your face and your jaw.

03

Step 3 of 5

Trial smile

You try the design in temporary teeth, so you can see, speak and chew with it before anything is final.

04

Step 4 of 5

Final restorations

The final crowns, bridges or implant teeth are made and fitted in stages, with the bite checked at each visit.

05

Step 5 of 5

Maintenance

Regular reviews and cleaning protect the work. A night guard may be advised if you grind your teeth.

Technology & innovation

Technology used

  • Panoramic X-rayRoutine X-rays are taken here during your visit, with no trip to a separate imaging centre.
  • Planning on screenYour options are talked through on screen, at a desk, before anything is decided.
  • SterilisationInstruments are cleaned and sterilised in their own room, away from where you are treated.
  • Instrument careHandpieces are maintained after use, and distilled water is made on site for the equipment.
