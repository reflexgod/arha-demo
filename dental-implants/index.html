Specialist care

Dental implants

Replacing missing teeth with results that look, feel and work like your own.

Book a consultationCall now

A tooth that feels like your own

An implant is a small post, usually titanium, that takes the place of a missing tooth’s root. Once it has settled into the jaw, a crown, bridge or denture is fixed on top.

Implant care at Arha is led by an ITI-certified prosthodontist, so the tooth you will see and use is planned first, and the implant is placed to support it.

Is this for you?

One missing tooth
A single implant and crown, without reshaping the healthy teeth on either side.
Several missing teeth
Implants can carry a bridge, so fewer implants replace more teeth.
Loose or uncomfortable dentures
A denture can be anchored to implants so it stays firmly in place.
A tooth that cannot be saved
When a tooth has to come out, its replacement can be planned at the same time.

How it works

Step by step, from the first conversation to the care that follows.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We listen to what you want and examine your mouth, with the X-rays or scans needed to plan safely.

  2. 02

    Treatment planning

    The final tooth is designed first, then the implant position is planned around it.

  3. 03

    Implant placement

    The implant is placed at a pace that keeps you comfortable. We explain beforehand how your smile will look while you heal.

  4. 04

    Healing

    The bone gradually bonds with the implant. We review how it is settling.

  5. 05

    Your final tooth

    The crown, bridge or denture is made to match your smile and fitted with your bite checked carefully.

  6. 06

    Aftercare

    Regular reviews and cleaning protect the implant and the gum around it.

Crown

02

Abutment

A small connector that joins the crown to the implant, at the gum line.

03

Implant

A titanium post that takes the place of the root and bonds with the bone as it heals.

04

Bone

The jawbone holds the implant firmly once it has healed around it.

Technology & innovation

Technology used

  • Panoramic X-ray Routine X-rays are taken here during your visit, with no trip to a separate imaging centre.
  • Treatment rooms You lie back comfortably, the work area is well lit, and your images can be shown to you as we explain.
  • Planning on screen Your options are talked through on screen, at a desk, before anything is decided.
  • Sterilisation Instruments are cleaned and sterilised in their own room, away from where you are treated.

Questions, answered

Most adults who have lost a tooth can be considered, but your gums, your bone and your general health all play a part. A consultation, with the X-rays or scans needed, is the only reliable way to know.

Implant treatment is carried out with anaesthetic so that you are comfortable. We will explain exactly what to expect during and after treatment at your consultation.

Implant treatment happens in stages, with healing time in between. How long depends on your bone and the teeth involved; you will get a personal timeline at your consultation.

Implants are a long-term replacement. Like natural teeth, they depend on good care at home and regular reviews, and the crown on top may need attention over the years.

Bone loss does not always rule implants out. We will explain the options suited to you once we have seen your X-rays or scans.
